# Short video scripts (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)

Format: vertical, 15–45 seconds, captions burned in. Tools: LumaFusion + StoryBlocks; the Mbraceables as plush or simple animation. Rules from the video guide: any number on screen shows its source and checked date in the corner. Characters speak feelings and next steps; a narrator or on-screen text carries facts.

---

### 1. "The deadline nobody told you about" (25s) · Max on camera

**0–3s** (Max, kitchen or truck) "If you heat with gas in Pepco or Delmarva territory, there's a date you should know."
**3–10s** On screen: *Nov 30, 2026. Higher switching amounts need the install finished.* Corner: *Source: Pepco HVAC Efficiency Program · checked Oct 16, 2026*
**10–18s** "I'm not selling you a heat pump. I built a free 2-minute check that shows which programs fit your home, with the official links."
**18–25s** Phone screen recording of the checker. "Link's in the bio. And send it to one neighbor."

### 2. Scout and the blower door (20s)

**0–4s** Scout peeks around a door frame. Scout: "Let's investigate where the air sneaks out."
**4–12s** Real footage: blower door fan, smoke pencil at a window. Text: *A blower door test finds the leaks.*
**12–20s** Text: *Seal first. Then size the heat pump.* Scout: "Mystery solved." End card: *dmvheatpumps.com/check*

### 3. Fluffle's warm hug (15s)

**0–5s** Fluffle wrapped in a tiny blanket. Fluffle: "Wrap your home in a warm hug."
**5–12s** Attic insulation footage. Text: *Insulate before you buy. A tighter home can need a smaller heat pump.*
**12–15s** End card.

### 4. Sparky's brave choice (20s)

**0–4s** Sparky at the edge of a "diving board" (a stack of books). Sparky: "Be brave. Make the change."
**4–14s** Text, one per beat: *1 Audit · 2 Seal · 3 Insulate · 4 Size it with a Manual J*
**14–20s** Sparky jumps, lands softly on a pillow. Text: *Brave choices, in the right order.* End card.

### 5. "The 25C credit is over" (15s) · myth-buster

**0–3s** Text slam: *That $2,000 heat pump tax credit?*
**3–9s** *Ended for systems placed in service after Dec 31, 2025.* Corner: *Source: IRS · checked Oct 16, 2026*
**9–15s** Max: "If your 2026 quote counts on it, ask why." End card: *12 questions to ask before you sign → link in bio.*

### 6. "Why I built this" (45s) · Max, from the mission page

"I spent years as a vascular ultrasound tech. Every decision started with an accurate measurement. Home energy works the same way: measure the house, seal it, size the system, then install.
Some companies in this industry are measured on ticket size and close rates. When those numbers come first, the homeowner comes second.
So we built the opposite. Every fact links to the official source, with the date we checked it. We'll tell you to fix the house first, even when it means a smaller system.
Community first. Success follows. If this helps you, send it to one neighbor."

## Trend formats that fit the brand

- **"Things my installer asked me that made me trust them"**: stitch-able. Invite installers to duet with their own answers to the 12 questions.
- **"Rating my neighbor's heat pump quote"** (with permission, numbers blurred): walk through the checklist on a real quote.
- **POV: a felt friend handed to a tech at dawn**: Act III of the concept. Pure feeling, no facts, end on "Craftsman of the Month nominations are open."
