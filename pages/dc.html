DC heat pump rebates for any income, and no-cost help if you qualify.

DCSEU lists rebates of $4,000 to $5,000 for switching from gas to an efficient heat pump, for installs Oct 1, 2026 to Sep 30, 2027. You need a DC master-licensed installer and a permit. Apply within 30 days.
