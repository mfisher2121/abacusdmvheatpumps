Before You Sign

Twelve questions to ask any heat pump installer, a one-page deadline calendar, and the envelope-first order. It fits on the fridge.

A heat pump is one of the biggest things most of us ever buy. The right questions protect your rebate, your comfort and your budget. Good installers are glad you asked.

A few of the questions

  • Are you a participating contractor in my utility's program? Which list are you on?
  • Will you list each incentive on the contract as its own line?
  • Did you do a room-by-room load calculation (Manual J)? Can I have a copy?
  • Are you counting on any federal tax credit? (In 2026, the right answer is no.)
