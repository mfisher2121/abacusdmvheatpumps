Where they came from

The Mbraceables began in 2023 as Kevin the Koala: Embracing His True Colors, a story about an overlooked kid who discovers his quiet gift is a superpower. That story, not a sales funnel, set the values of everything we build: shared success, every voice heard, and the magic that happens when different gifts come together.

“True success is shared success.”

Our promises about the Mbraceables

  • They never state rebate amounts, deadlines or rules. Those come only from official sources.
  • They never endorse a business.
  • We don't market to children or collect data from children.
  • Plush friends ship only after real toy-safety testing.

Craftsman of the Month

Know someone who does it right?

Each month we tell the story of one local owner or tech, chosen for character, not ad spend. The one who came back on a Saturday. The one who told you that you didn't need the bigger system. The one who left the attic cleaner than they found it.

Homeowners and fellow pros can nominate. We only share names, stories or photos with everyone's written consent.
