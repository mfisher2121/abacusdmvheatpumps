Common questions

Is there a federal tax credit for heat pumps in 2026?

No. The IRS says the 25C credit can't be claimed for property placed in service after Dec 31, 2025.

When does Pepco's bigger heat pump amount end?

Installs must be finished between May 15 and Nov 30, 2026. It's for switching from gas, oil or propane to an air-source heat pump. Delmarva Power has the same window.

Will EmPOWER rebates exist in 2027?

The law keeps EmPOWER, with smaller targets for 2027 to 2029. The PSC is reviewing the utilities' plans (hearings Oct 26 to 29, 2026). Amounts aren't decided.

Do I need an energy audit to get a rebate?

Not for the utility HVAC programs. You do for Home Performance with ENERGY STAR, which has the highest limits (up to $15,000 with a fuel switch) and needs air sealing and insulation.

Does BEPS apply to my house?

No. Maryland BEPS covers commercial and apartment buildings of 35,000 sq ft or more. Building guide →
