In effect

Maryland BEPS

Maryland's Building Energy Performance Standards cover commercial and apartment buildings of 35,000 sq ft or more. Owners report energy use every year and must meet emissions limits starting in 2030. It does not apply to houses.

Montgomery County says its buildings are exempt from the state program because of HB 49, and follow only the county's own rules.

Sources: MDE: BEPS · Montgomery County BEPS, checked Oct 15, 2026

In effect

DC BEPS

From the Clean Energy DC Omnibus Act of 2018. The goal is to cut greenhouse gases and energy use 50% by 2032. Buildings over 10,000 sq ft must report energy and water use.

Affordable buildings with 5+ units may qualify for the Affordable Housing Retrofit Accelerator (AHRA).

Sources: DOEE: BEPS · DCSEU: Retrofit Accelerator, checked {{CHECKED_DC}}

Different

Northern Virginia

We found no building performance standard in force in Northern Virginia. Dominion runs programs for property managers.

Source: Dominion: property management programs, checked {{CHECKED_VA}}
