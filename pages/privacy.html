Privacy notice · Last updated Oct 19, 2026

Your information, your yes.

We collect as little as we can, we never sell it, and we don't share it with anyone unless you say yes to that specific sharing.

What we collect, and why

  • Rebate checker answers. Your answers stay in your browser to show results. If you choose to email your summary, we receive your answers (not your income) with your email, to send it.
  • Email, and ZIP if you give it. To send what you asked for: a summary, a checklist, alerts or a newsletter. Each is a separate checkbox, never pre-checked.
  • Mobile number, only if you choose texts. Texts are a separate yes, never required. Reply STOP to cancel.
  • Consent records. When you say yes, we keep the time, the page, and the exact words you agreed to, so we can prove we only contact people who asked.
  • A random share code. If you share your link, it carries a short random code so we can count neighbors helped. It does not identify the people you share with.
  • Page counts. We use cookieless, aggregate analytics. No ad pixels, no session replay, no tracking across other sites.

Who we share with

  • Installers, only with your yes. If you ask to be matched, we show you the installers' names first. Nothing is shared until you check the box naming them. Some installers pay for matched requests, and we tell you which.
  • Service providers who send our emails and texts and host this site, only to do that work for us.
  • We never sell your personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your information, or withdraw any consent, at any time. Email {{EMAIL}}. Withdrawing is as easy as saying yes: one click in any email, or STOP by text.

Children

This site is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

How long we keep it

Alert and newsletter subscribers: until you unsubscribe. Installer match requests: 24 months, then deleted. Consent records: as long as the law requires.
