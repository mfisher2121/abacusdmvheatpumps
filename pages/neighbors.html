Host a 30-minute porch talk

Coffee, a few neighbors, one laptop. No sales pitches at the door.

  1. 5 min: Why now. Pull up the dates we're watching on a phone or TV.
  2. 10 min: Everyone runs the 2-minute check for their own home.
  3. 10 min: Walk through the Before You Sign questions together.
  4. 5 min: Swap notes: who has had an audit, which installers did a Manual J.
